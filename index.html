How it interacts with T1 / T2
Trailing arms when price reaches your When to start trailing level (entry + R × risk) — a reference only, never a target. From then Stockkar raises your stop to the daily EMA minus this %, and the stop never moves down.
T1 and T2 are always real broker orders and book as usual; the trail lifts the stop on the live legs (like Move SL to Cost, but it keeps climbing) and runs until T1 or T2 fires — or the trailed stop, if the trend turns first.

The stop never moves down, so the runner exits at T2 if it gets there, or at the trailed stop if the trend turns first.

Broker-Side Risk Control
Dhan is built for algo traders who need clean order execution.

Stockkar works especially well with Dhan because Super Orders can carry entry, stop-loss, target, and trailing logic in one execution flow. That gives the user a cleaner path from screener signal to managed trade.

Open Dhan Account
Super OrderEntry, stop-loss, target, and trailing fields can be prepared together for a disciplined trade plan.
Trail LogicTrailing stop-loss support helps protect gains when price moves in favour of the trade.
Static IPDhanHQ supports static-IP based API access, matching the AWS backend setup used by this app.
Cleaner LogsOrder IDs and broker responses are easier to trace when orders are routed through one broker flow.

Why Super Orders Matter

A normal order only places the entry. A Super Order lets the algo send the entry along with exit planning, so the trade has a defined target and risk point from the beginning.

Better Fit For Automation

For scheduled algos, broker-side order structure reduces manual handling after entry. Stockkar can focus on signal scanning, filtering, and order placement.

Practical Risk Discipline

Stop-loss, target, risk-reward, and trailing settings are visible before the order is placed, helping users avoid accidental open-ended trades.

How It Works With Stockkar
Fetch ScreenerUser selects built-in or saved Stockkar screener and fetches latest stocks.
Configure AlgoUser sets entry filters, sector/industry filters, SL, target, and risk-reward.
Start AlgoAWS backend monitors during the selected window and checks criteria repeatedly.
Place Super OrderWhen criteria match, Stockkar routes the prepared trade to Dhan with managed exit logic.
Trading involves risk. Super Orders and trailing logic help structure execution, but they do not guarantee profit or prevent all losses. Users should test with small quantities first.

This page explains how this personal Stockkar Algo app is provided and how responsibility is handled.
Personal App Ownership
This Stockkar Algo application is a personal, user-owned app configured for your individual use. You have full control over your server, broker connection, tokens, screeners, criteria, and order execution settings.

We provide software development, setup assistance, and technical implementation services. We do not operate a shared trading platform, pooled execution service, portfolio management service, investment advisory desk, or broker-managed account on your behalf.

The app runs for you using the settings, broker credentials, and trading rules that you choose to save inside your own setup.

User Responsibility
You create or select screenersYou decide which Stockkar built-in or saved screeners should be used for scanning stocks.
You define entry and exit criteriaYou choose filters, indicators, stop-loss, target, risk-reward, quantity, sector, industry, and schedule settings.
You connect your own brokerYou authorise your personal broker account by saving API keys, tokens, static IP, callback URLs, and other required broker details.
You approve algo deploymentYou start, pause, resume, cancel, test, or run algos from your own app interface.
Trading Risk
Equity and derivatives trading involve market risk. Automated execution can create losses due to market movement, liquidity, slippage, broker rejection, token expiry, internet/server issues, exchange rules, or incorrect user configuration.

You are fully responsible for reviewing every screener, strategy rule, broker permission, order type, quantity, and risk setting before enabling live execution.

You should test with Test Mode or very small quantities first and monitor live orders directly inside your broker account.

No Advisory Or Guaranteed Returns
We do not provide buy/sell recommendations, profit guarantees, assured returns, portfolio management, or discretionary trading decisions.
The app only executes the technical workflow configured by you, from your own screeners and rules, through your own broker account.
